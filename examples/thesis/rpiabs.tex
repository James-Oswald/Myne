\specialhead{ABSTRACT}
 
The topic of inetlligence has long been of intrest to humans, from Aristotle, to Bennet, and now to the age of AI. In this work, we undertake a mathmatical investigation of machine intelligence, the limits of machine intelligence, and the possibility of machine intelligence. In particular, we explore the subsumption relations between existing intelligence relations, the consequences of various impossibility results from social choice theory on intelligence, and explore a logic based model of intelligence.
