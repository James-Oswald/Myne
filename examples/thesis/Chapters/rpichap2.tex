\chapter{Machine Intelligence Measures}

This variation is reflected in the formal-measures literature, which contains several
families of measures. Performance-based measures understand intelligence as goal
achievement across a broad class of environments, and are most directly connected to the
extension and transfer questions. Psychometric and test-based measures understand
intelligence as performance on constructed cognitive tasks, and are relevant to
questions about transfer and aggregation. Skill-acquisition measures understand
intelligence as efficient generalization from limited experience, connecting the
extension, transfer, and structure questions. Structural and cognitive measures
understand intelligence as possession of reasoning, representation, or architecture, and
are especially relevant to the question of when behavioral success implies internal
structure.
